Game description

VOID PILOT 3D: ROGUE GALAXY is an indie sci-fi action game with a retro arcade look. Explore strange worlds, face hostile threats, and upgrade your ship as you push deeper into the galaxy.

Try the free 10-minute browser demo and get a taste of the action. It runs directly in your browser on PC or Android, with touch, keyboard and gamepad support. Built entirely on an Android phone.

What’s new: major update

October 2026. New worlds, new dangers and a place to call home. Here’s what just landed in VOID PILOT 3D.

🌩️

Weather on every world New

Storms on Home World, lightning on Plasma World, a Singularity anomaly on Void World, blizzards on Cryo World, meteorite showers on Iron World and solar flares on Titanium World. Every planet has its own hazards, and its own ways to die. New planet globes too.

🏠

Home Sweet Home New

Your own house on Home World with a landing pad, a bed (sleep to heal), sofa, trophy shelf, lava lamp and windows onto the world outside. Plus three arcade cabinets to play: Asteroid Breaker, Galaxy Dive and Uranus Destroyers.

🏴‍☠️

Pirate bunkers New

Find the entrance on the planet surface, ride the lift down and fight through sentinels, flame jets, mines and tripwires. Collect keycards, crack the vaults and take on the Void Warlord.

🔫

New weapons & tougher enemies New

Super Shotgun, Heavy Pulse rifle and Plasma Cutter, with new first-person art. Enemies now attack with their own patterns: acid, fireballs and a boss with moves of his own.

🎨

Suit colours New

Choose Original, Neon Rave or Stealth from the panel on your gear rack. Your suit colour shows everywhere, even in the cutscenes.

📋

Contracts, log & more New

Take contracts at the shop (ore deliveries, hunts), keep your story in the Captain’s Log, go on space walks, and dodge the moving laser grids inside derelict dreadnoughts. Tuned to run smoothly on budget phones.
